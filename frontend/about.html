About Learning Management System


A Learning Management System (LMS) is a digital platform used by educational institutions to provide learning resources, assignments, communication and academic activities to students.



LMS in Higher Education


📚 Online Learning

Students can access notes, videos and other learning resources online.

📝 Assignments

Students can receive and submit assignments through the LMS.

💬 Communication

Teachers can communicate important information and announcements to students.

📊 Progress Tracking

Students can monitor their academic activities and learning progress.
